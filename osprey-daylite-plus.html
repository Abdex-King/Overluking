Osprey Daylite Plus

An honest review of one of the most popular daypacks on the market. What owners actually say — the good, the bad, and who it's for.

The Osprey Daylite Plus is one of the most reviewed daypacks you can buy. Over 3,700 Amazon reviewers have rated it 4.6 out of 5. That kind of number usually means one of two things: it's genuinely excellent, or the reviews are inflated. We read through hundreds of them to figure out which.

The short version

This is a 20-liter daypack designed for short hikes, day trips, commuting, and travel. It weighs about 1.5 pounds, has a ventilated back panel, two deep water-bottle pockets, and a laptop sleeve. It costs around $80 and is covered by Osprey's lifetime warranty.

It's excellent for what it is. It's not for everyone.

Our verdict

Buy the Osprey Daylite Plus if you want a light, well-organized daypack for hikes, commutes, and short trips. Skip it if you need a backpacking pack, or if you want a bag that stands up on its own.

Who it's for

  • Day hikers. It holds everything you need for a 5–10 mile hike: water, snacks, a light jacket, first aid, and a map.
  • Commuters. It has a padded laptop sleeve and enough pockets to keep your work things organized.
  • Travelers. It fits under most airplane seats and doubles as a day bag once you arrive.
  • Anyone who wants one pack that does a lot. Owners repeatedly say it became their "daily" or "everyday" bag.

Who it's not for

  • Backpackers on overnight trips. At 20 liters, it's too small for a sleeping bag and tent.
  • People who want a bag that stands up on its own. Multiple owners complain it flops over.
  • Anyone expecting exactly 30 liters. Despite some listings saying 30L, the actual capacity is 20L.
  • Heavy-load haulers. The hip belt is thin — it's for light loads only.

The good

  • Light for its size. At about 1.5 lbs, you won't notice it on your back.
  • Excellent organization. Multiple pockets and a laptop sleeve mean everything has a place.
  • Deep bottle pockets. They hold 32 oz bottles without stretching.
  • Ventilated back panel. Owners say it stays cool on warm hikes.
  • Osprey's lifetime warranty. If anything breaks, Osprey fixes or replaces it.
  • Widely loved. 3,766 reviews, 4.6 stars, and people keep buying more.

The not-so-good

  • It won't stand up on its own. This is the most common complaint. If you like setting your bag on the ground and having it stay upright, this one flops over.
  • Smaller than the listing says. Some listings show 30L, but the actual capacity is 20L.
  • Price feels high to some. At around $80, some reviewers feel it's overpriced compared to alternatives.
  • Occasional quality issues. A small number of owners report zipper pulls tearing after a year or so. Osprey's warranty covers these.

How it compares

Osprey Daylite Plus vs. Deuter Trail 25

If you're willing to spend more, the Deuter Trail 25 is a step up. It costs around $135 — nearly $60 more than the Osprey — but you get a more structured frame, an Airstripes back system that keeps the pack close to your body, and better load distribution for longer days. The Osprey is lighter and better for light loads; the Deuter Trail 25 is the better pick if you plan to carry more or hike further.

Osprey Daylite Plus vs. REI Co-op Trail 25

The REI pack is a similar size (25L) and a similar price (~$80), but it's not sold on Amazon. It includes a raincover — a real advantage if you hike in wet conditions — and has a slightly more structured back. If you're an REI member and prefer shopping there, the Trail 25 is a good alternative to the Osprey Daylite Plus.

FAQ

Is it really 20 liters or 30 liters?

The actual capacity is 20 liters. Some listings show 30L, which is wrong. If you need 30 liters, look at a different pack entirely.

Does it fit a laptop?

Yes — it has a padded laptop sleeve that fits most 14" laptops. Some owners fit 16" models in with a squeeze.

Is it waterproof?

No. It repels light rain, but the fabric isn't fully waterproof. For serious rain, buy a raincover.

How much does it weigh?

About 1.5 pounds (0.7 kg).

What's the warranty?

Osprey offers a lifetime warranty against defects. If a zipper breaks or stitching fails, they repair or replace it.

The verdict

If you need a light, well-organized daypack for hikes, commutes, and short trips, the Osprey Daylite Plus is one of the safest buys you can make. It's not flashy. It won't stand up on its own. It won't carry a sleeping bag. But it does its job extremely well, and thousands of owners agree.

If you fit into that category, this is an easy recommendation.
