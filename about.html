About

We are the ones looking out over something big.

Overluking is a small outfit for people who would rather be outside than almost anywhere else. Here is what we do and why.

The short version

Overluking started with a habit: driving until the road ran out, then walking a little further to see what was around the next ridge. Somewhere along the way, that habit turned into a name for the moment you stop climbing and start looking.

It is the pullout at first light. The ridge after the switchbacks. The quiet minute before anyone says a word.

What we cover

We write about the trails, gear, and trips that get you to those moments, and we keep it simple enough to start this weekend. That means beginner-friendly trail guides, honest gear picks, and a handful of trips worth the drive.

How we pick gear

We do not test every product ourselves. Instead, we read what owners, testers, and long-term reviewers report, and we describe what a piece of gear is actually good for. When we recommend something, we say why. When a product is not worth it, we say that too.

How we make money

Some links on Overluking are affiliate links. If you buy through one, we may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. That is how the site stays free to read. We only link to products we would suggest to a friend.

You can read the full affiliate disclosure if you want the fine print.

Who this is for

If you have ever stood somewhere high and quiet and thought "I should do this more," this is for you. Whether you are planning your first five-mile hike or your tenth trip out west, we hope there is something here that helps.

See you out there.
